# Material Icons

Back navigation controls and the routine days dropdown use Google's Material Icons `arrow_back` and `expand_more` SVGs.

- Source: https://github.com/google/material-design-icons/blob/master/src/navigation/arrow_back/materialicons/24px.svg
- Source: https://github.com/google/material-design-icons/blob/master/src/navigation/expand_more/materialicons/24px.svg

- License: [material-icons-LICENSE.txt](material-icons-LICENSE.txt)

The `arrow-forward` symbol reuses `arrow_back`, rotated 180 degrees.
